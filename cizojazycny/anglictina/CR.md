# The Czech Republic

## Geography

* Located in **Central Europe**; often referred to as the "heart of Europe".
* **Landlocked country** (surrounded entirely by land with no coastline).
* Borders four neighbouring countries: Germany, Poland, Slovakia, Austria
* Composed of three historical regions: Bohemia, Moravia, Czech Silesia
* Total surface area: **78,866 km²**.

### Mountain Ranges and Peaks

* The country's borders are formed by a natural ring of mountain ranges:
  * **Krkonoše (Giant Mountains):** Located in the north along the Polish border; contains **Sněžka** (1,603 m), the highest peak in the country.
  * **Šumava (Bohemian Forest):** Located in the south-west along the German and Austrian borders; heavily forested with glacial lakes.
  * **Krušné hory (Ore Mountains):** Located in the north-west along the Saxon border.
  * **Jizerské and Orlické hory:** Located in northern and north-eastern Bohemia.
  * **Hrubý Jeseník:** Located in northern Moravia (highest peak: Praděd, 1,491 m).
  * **Moravskoslezské Beskydy:** Mountain range in eastern Moravia.

### Lowlands and Highlands

* **Bohemian-Moravian Highlands (*Českomoravská vrchovina* / Vysočina):** Rolling hilly region in the center.
* **Polabí (Elbe Lowland):** Fertile agricultural lowland along the Elbe river.
* **Hornomoravský and Dolnomoravský úval:** Fertile lowlands in central and southern Moravia.

### Rivers and Drainage ("The Roof of Europe")

* Called the "main European drainage divide" because rivers flow into three different seas:
  * **North Sea:** Drained by the **Elbe** (*Labe*), which starts in Krkonoše and merges with the Vltava at Mělník.
  * **Baltic Sea:** Drained by the **Oder** (*Odra*), which flows through Silesia into Poland.
  * **Black Sea:** Drained by the **Morava** river, which joins the Danube in Slovakia.
* **Vltava (Moldau):** Longest river entirely within the country (433 km); runs through Český Krumlov and Prague; features major reservoirs (Lipno, Orlík, Slapy).

### Lakes and Ponds

* Natural lakes are rare, mostly small glacial lakes in Šumava (Černé and Čertovo jezero).
* Extensive history of artificial **fish ponds** dating back to the 16th century.
* Concentrated in the **Třeboň Basin** in South Bohemia (e.g. Rožmberk, the largest pond in Central Europe, built by Jakub Krčín).

## Climate and Weather

* **Temperate continental climate** transitioning between maritime Atlantic and dry eastern European weather.
* Characterized by **four distinct seasons**.

## Population and Minorities

### Demographics

* Total population: approx. **10.8 million people**.
* Heavily concentrated in major cities:
  * **Prague:** Capital city (approx. 1.35 million).
  * **Brno:** Largest city in Moravia (approx. 400,000).
  * **Ostrava:** Industrial centre of Silesia (approx. 280,000).
  * **Plzeň:** Capital of West Bohemia, famous for brewing (approx. 180,000).

### Ethnic Groups and Minorities

* Relatively homogeneous Slavic society; majority identify as Czech, Moravian, or Silesian.
* **Traditional minorities:**
  * **Slovaks:** Largest traditional minority, sharing a common history and close language.
  * **Poles:** Concentrated in the Těšín region in Silesia.
  * **Germans:** Small historic minority remaining after post-WWII expulsions.
  * **Roma community:** Distinct cultural and ethnic community.
* **Recent immigrant communities:**
  * **Ukrainians:** Largest recent immigrant population.
  * **Vietnamese:** Well-integrated community originally invited during the socialist era.

### Religion and Society

* One of the most **secular and atheist** countries in the world.
* The majority of people declare no religious affiliation.
* Roman Catholicism is the largest traditional church, with higher practice in southern Moravia.

## Economy

### Economic Standing

* Developed, export-oriented industrial economy.
* Member of the **European Union** (joined 2004) and OECD; uses the **Czech Koruna (CZK)** as currency.

### Automotive Industry

* The backbone of the national economy (generates roughly 20% of industrial output).
* **Škoda Auto** (based in Mladá Boleslav, part of the Volkswagen Group).
* Major foreign manufacturing plants: Hyundai (Nošovice) and Toyota (Kolín).

### Mechanical Engineering and High-Tech

* Heavy machinery, transport equipment (trams and trains by Škoda Transportation)
* Firearm production: Česká zbrojovka (Colt CZ Group)
* Optics and electron microscopes (Brno is a global hub)
* Software and cybersecurity: Avast, JetBrains, Gen Digital, Pruša

### Traditional Crafts and Products

* **Bohemian crystal and glassware:** World-renowned glass factories (Moser in Karlovy Vary, Preciosa in Jablonec).
* Porcelain (Karlovarský porcelán / Thun).
* Musical instruments: Petrof upright and grand pianos in Hradec Králové.
* Beer brewing: World-famous Czech lagers.

### Natural Resources and Agriculture

* Limited raw minerals: brown coal (North Bohemia), kaolin (for porcelain), uranium, and lithium deposits.
* Agricultural crops: wheat, barley, sugar beet, potatoes, oilseed rape, and world-class **Žatec hops**.
* Animal farming: dairy cows, cattle, pigs, and freshwater carp breeding.

### Services and Foreign Trade

* Services generate around 60% of GDP (finance, IT, logistics, tourism).
* **Germany** is the single largest trading partner (absorbs over 30% of Czech exports).

## Political System

### Form of Government

* **Sovereign, parliamentary republic** with a representative democracy.
* Based on the Constitution adopted in December 1992.

### Legislative Branch (The Parliament)

* Bicameral parliament consisting of two chambers:
  * **Chamber of Deputies (*Poslanecká sněmovna*):**
    * 200 members (MPs) elected for 4 years via proportional representation.
    * Proposes, debates, and votes on laws; approves national budget; votes confidence in the government.
  * **Senate (*Senát*):**
    * 81 senators elected for 6 years (one-third renewed every 2 years) in single-seat districts.
    * Serves as a constitutional safeguard; can delay or amend laws passed by the deputies.

### Executive Branch

* **The President (Head of State):**
  * Currently **Petr Pavel**.
  * Directly elected by the public for a 5-year term (maximum two terms).
  * Ceremonial head of state and commander-in-chief of armed forces.
  * Appoints the Prime Minister, judges, and members of the Czech National Bank board; holds a suspensive veto over laws.
* **The Government (Prime Minister and Cabinet):**
  * Holds the main executive power.
  * Led by the Prime Minister; must hold the confidence of the Chamber of Deputies.

### Judicial Branch

* Independent court system: District, Regional, High Courts, and Supreme Court.
* **Constitutional Court (*Ústavní soud*)** in Brno protects basic constitutional rights and reviews the legality of laws.

## Sightseeing in the Czech Republic

### Český Krumlov (South Bohemia)

* UNESCO World Heritage site built in a scenic loop of the Vltava River.
* Monumental medieval and Renaissance castle complex with a preserved Baroque castle theatre.
* Cobbled streets, historic town houses, and revolving open-air theatre in the castle gardens.

### Karlovy Vary (West Bohemia)

* World-famous spa town founded in the 14th century by King Charles IV.
* Known for 12 hot mineral springs (including the powerful *Vřídlo* geyser).
* Traditional spa colonnades, Moser crystal, spa wafers (*oplatky*), herbal liqueur **Becherovka**, and the International Film Festival.

### Karlštejn Castle (Central Bohemia)

* High Gothic fortress built in 1348 by Emperor Charles IV.
* Constructed to protect the Holy Roman imperial crown jewels and Czech royal relics.
* Famous Chapel of the Holy Cross decorated with Gothic panel paintings by Master Theodoric.

### Kutná Hora (Central Bohemia)

* Historic medieval silver mining town that once rivalled Prague in wealth.
* **St Barbara's Cathedral:** Magnificent late Gothic church dedicated to the patron saint of miners.
* **Sedlec Ossuary:** Bone church decorated with the bones of tens of thousands of human skeletons.

### Lednice-Valtice Cultural Landscape (South Moravia)

* UNESCO-protected romantic landscape park designed by the Princes of Liechtenstein.
* Features the neo-Gothic Lednice Castle, grand greenhouse, Turkish minaret, and Valtice Baroque palace.
* Surrounded by vineyards, ponds, and romantic forest follies.

## Miscellanea

### National Flag and Symbols

* **The Flag:**
  * Two equal horizontal stripes: **white** (top) and **red** (bottom).
  * A **blue triangle** at the hoist side reaching the middle.
  * Originally adopted in 1920 for Czechoslovakia; retained by the Czech Republic in 1993.
* **National Anthem:** *"Kde domov můj?"* (Where is my home?), composed by František Škroup with lyrics by Josef Kajetán Tyl.
* **State Motto:** *"Pravda vítězí"* (Truth prevails).
* **National Tree:** The **Linden tree** (*lípa*), representing Slavic roots and protection.

### The Coat of Arms

* Quartered shield displaying the historic heraldic animals:
  * 1st and 4th fields: Silver **double-tailed crowned lion** on red (Bohemia).
  * 2nd field: Red-and-silver chequered **eagle** on blue (Moravia).
  * 3rd field: Black **eagle** with a silver crescent on gold (Silesia).

### Famous Historical Personalities

* **Charles IV:** 14th-century Holy Roman Emperor and Czech King, founded Charles University and built Charles Bridge (*Father of the Homeland*).
* **Jan Hus:** 15th-century church reformer burned at the stake in 1415.
* **Jan Amos Comenius (*Komenský*):** 17th-century philosopher and pioneer of modern education (*Teacher of Nations*).
* **Franz Kafka:** World-famous Prague German writer (*The Metamorphosis*, *The Trial*).
* **Karel Čapek:** Playwright who gave the world the word **"robot"** in his drama *R.U.R.*
* **Otto Wichterle:** Chemist who invented modern soft contact lenses.
* **Václav Havel:** Dissident playwright, leader of the Velvet Revolution, and first post-communist President.

### Czech Food and Beer Culture

* **Highest beer consumption:** The Czech Republic ranks #1 in the world in beer consumption per capita (~130–140 litres per person per year).
* **Pilsner lager:** Invented in Plzeň in 1842 (*Pilsner Urquell*), the original bottom-fermented golden beer.
* **Traditional cuisine:** Hearty food served with bread or potato dumplings (*knedlíky*):
  * *Vepřo-knedlo-zelo:* Roast pork with dumplings and sauerkraut.
  * *Svíčková na smetaně:* Beef sirloin in vegetable cream sauce with cranberries.
  * *Guláš:* Beef stew with onions and paprika.

### Sports Achievements

* **Ice Hockey:** National sport with historic victories:
  * Olympic Gold at **Nagano 1998** ("The Tournament of the Century").
  * Multiple World Championship titles (most recently Prague 2024).
  * Legends: **Jaromír Jágr** (second in all-time NHL points) and **Dominik Hašek** (legendary goaltender).
* **Football (Soccer):** Silver medal at Euro 1996, bronze at Euro 2004; Ballon d'Or winners Josef Masopust and Pavel Nedvěd.
* **Athletics and Tennis:** Olympic champion Emil Zátopek (three gold medals at 1952 Helsinki Games), Jan Železný (javelin world record), and Wimbledon champions (Martina Navrátilová, Petra Kvitová, Barbora Krejčíková).
