# Prague

## Overview: The Capital City

### Basic Facts and Nicknames
* Capital and largest city of the Czech Republic.
* Located in Central Bohemia along both banks of the **Vltava River**.
* Population: approx. **1.35 million people**.
* Historic city centre has been a **UNESCO World Heritage site** since 1992.
* Famous nicknames:
  * *"The City of a Hundred Spires"* (*Stověžatá Praha* - coined in the 19th century; today has thousands).
  * *"The Golden City"* (associated with gilded towers and alchemists).
  * *"The Heart of Europe"*.

### Political and Administrative Role
* Seat of the President of the Republic (Prague Castle).
* Seat of the Czech Parliament:
  * Chamber of Deputies and Senate located in the historic palaces of Malá Strana.
* Seat of the Government (Straka Academy) and foreign embassies.

### Education and Economy
* Generates over 25% of national GDP; major finance, tourism, and technology center.
* Oldest university in Central Europe: **Charles University** (founded in 1348 by Charles IV).
* Other major universities: Czech Technical University (ČVUT), Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE).

## The Old Town (*Staré Město*)

### Old Town Square (*Staroměstské náměstí*)
* Historical heart of Prague dating back to the 11th century as a market square.
* Surrounded by Romanesque, Gothic, Renaissance, and Baroque buildings.
* Centre features the large bronze **Jan Hus Memorial** (unveiled in 1915).

### The Astronomical Clock (*Pražský orloj*)
* Located on the southern wall of the **Old Town Hall** (built in 1410).
* Every hour, visitors watch the procession of the **Twelve Apostles**.
* Outer moving figures include Death ringing a bell, Vanity, Greed, and a Turk.
* Displays astronomical time, zodiac signs, and positions of the sun and moon.

### Church of Our Lady before Týn
* Iconic Gothic church dominating the square with twin 80-metre spires.
* Burial site of the famous 16th-century Danish astronomer **Tycho Brahe**.

### Charles Bridge (*Karlův most*)
* Historic stone bridge connecting the Old Town with the Lesser Town.
* Founded in **1357** by Emperor Charles IV; designed by Petr Parléř.
* Length: 516 metres; supported by 16 stone arches.
* Lined with **30 Baroque statues** of saints:
  * Most famous: **St John of Nepomuk** (touching his plaque brings good luck).
* Guarded by three medieval fortified bridge towers.

### The Royal Route (*Královská cesta*)
* Historic coronation procession path for Bohemian kings.
* Starts at the **Powder Gate (*Prašná brána*)** and Municipal House.
* Passes through Celetná Street, Old Town Square, across Charles Bridge, through Malá Strana, and up to St Vitus Cathedral.

## The Jewish Quarter (*Josefov*)

### History of the Ghetto
* Originally a segregated walled medieval Jewish ghetto.
* Renamed *Josefov* in 1850 after Emperor **Joseph II**, who granted religious tolerance to Jews.
* Most of the old slum was demolished around 1900 and replaced by Art Nouveau boulevards (Pařížská street).
* Survived WWII because Nazis intended to create an "exotic museum of an extinct race".

### Old-New Synagogue (*Staronová synagoga*)
* Built around 1270; the oldest active synagogue in Central Europe.
* Early Gothic stone structure.
* **Legend of the Golem:** Folklore says Rabbi Loew created a clay giant to defend the Jewish community; the remains of the Golem are said to rest in the attic.

### Old Jewish Cemetery
* Operated from the 15th to the 18th century.
* Due to lack of space, graves were buried on top of each other in layers.
* Contains roughly 12,000 visible leaning gravestones, including Rabbi Loew's tomb.

### Memorial Synagogues
* **Pinkas Synagogue:** Interior walls are inscribed with the names of nearly 80,000 Czech and Moravian Holocaust victims.
* **Spanish Synagogue:** Magnificent Moorish-revival interior with golden arabesques and stained glass.

## The Lesser Town (*Malá Strana*)

### Atmosphere and Architecture
* Located on the river slopes directly below Prague Castle.
* Rebuilt in grand **Baroque style** after destructive fires in the 16th century.
* Filled with cobbled streets, quiet gardens, and foreign embassies.

### Church of St Nicholas (*Kostel sv. Mikuláše*)
* Masterpiece of High Baroque architecture designed by the Dientzenhofer family.
* Features a massive green copper dome, a 79-metre belfry, and ceiling frescoes.

### Infant Jesus of Prague (*Pražské Jezulátko*)
* Located inside the Church of Our Lady Victorious.
* 16th-century Spanish wax statue of baby Jesus.
* Pilgrimage site visited by believers from all over the world; dressed in ornate ceremonial robes.

### Wallenstein Palace and Parliament
* **Wallenstein Palace (*Valdštejnský palác*):** Massive early Baroque complex built by military commander Albrecht von Wallenstein; now the seat of the **Czech Senate**.
* Features Italianate gardens with fountains, peacocks, and an artificial stalactite wall (*grotto*).
* **Thun Palace:** Seat of the **Chamber of Deputies**.

### Kampa Island and Lennon Wall
* Peaceful island along the Vltava separated by the Čertovka canal ("Prague Venice").
* **John Lennon Wall:** Covered in graffiti, Beatles lyrics, and peace symbols; was a symbol of youth resistance against communism during the 1980s.

### Petřín Hill
* Large forested hill offering great panoramic views.
* **Petřín Lookout Tower:** 63-metre steel tower built for the 1891 Exhibition, inspired by the Eiffel Tower.
* Features a mirror maze and a funicular railway.

## Prague Castle (*Pražský hrad*)

### Significance and Size
* Founded around **880 AD** by Prince Bořivoj of the Přemyslid Dynasty.
* Officially the **largest coherent ancient castle complex in the world** (occupies around 70,000 m²).
* Historic seat of Bohemian monarchs, Holy Roman Emperors, and modern Czech presidents.

### St Vitus Cathedral (*Katedrála sv. Víta*)
* The spiritual center of the nation and a masterpiece of Gothic architecture.
* Construction began in **1344** under Charles IV (architects Matthias of Arras and Petr Parléř) and was finished in **1929**.
* **St Wenceslas Chapel:** Decorated with semi-precious stones and medieval frescoes; tomb of Saint Wenceslas.
* **Crown Chamber:** Locked room guarding the **Czech Crown Jewels** (requires seven keys held by top state and church officials).

### Old Royal Palace and Vladislav Hall
* Seat of Bohemian rulers until the 16th century.
* **Vladislav Hall:** Massive late Gothic vault with no interior columns; was used for coronation banquets and indoor horse jousting tournaments.
* **Second Defenestration of Prague (1618):** Protestant nobles threw Catholic governors from the palace window, triggering the Thirty Years' War.

### St George's Basilica
* The oldest surviving church inside the castle complex (founded around 920).
* Romanesque interior with twin stone towers (*Adam and Eve*) and a red Baroque facade.

### Golden Lane (*Zlatá ulička*)
* Picturesque row of tiny 16th-century houses built into the castle ramparts.
* Originally housed castle marksmen and goldsmiths.
* Writer **Franz Kafka** lived and worked at house No. 22 in 1916–1917.

## The New Town (*Nové Město*)

### Foundation by Charles IV
* Founded in **1348** by Emperor Charles IV to expand Prague.
* Laid out with wide streets and three massive market squares.

### Wenceslas Square (*Václavské náměstí*)
* 750-metre-long boulevard and the modern center of the city.
* Dominated by the neo-Renaissance **National Museum (*Národní muzeum*)**.
* Features the famous bronze **equestrian statue of St Wenceslas** by J. V. Myslbek.
* Site of major historical demonstrations:
  * Declaration of Czechoslovak independence in 1918.
  * Protests and self-immolation of Jan Palach in 1969.
  * Mass rallies of the 1989 **Velvet Revolution**.

### National Theatre (*Národní divadlo*)
* Neo-Renaissance building on the Vltava bank.
* Built from nationwide public donations in 1881 under the motto *"Národ sobě"* ("A nation unto itself").
* Burned down shortly after opening and was rebuilt within two years.

### Dancing House (*Tančící dům*)
* Modern deconstructivist building designed by Vlado Milunić and Frank Gehry (nicknamed "Fred and Ginger").
* Completed in 1996; symbolizes dynamic change after the fall of communism.

## Prague Zoo

### Location and Global Ranking
* Located in the green Troja basin in northern Prague, near the Troja Chateau.
* Opened in 1931; consistently ranked among the **top 5 zoos in the world**.

### Pavilions and Conservation
* Spans nearly 60 hectares of hills, meadows, and riverbank.
* Famous pavilions: *Indonesian Jungle*, *Elephant Valley*, *African Savannah*, *Gorilla Pavilion*.
* World-famous for saving and breeding the endangered **Przewalski's Horse** and returning them to the wild in Mongolia.

## Miscellanea

### Cultural Life
* **Classical Music:**
  * W. A. Mozart loved Prague and premiered his opera *Don Giovanni* at the Estates Theatre (*Stavovské divadlo*) in 1787.
  * **Prague Spring Festival (*Pražské jaro*):** Prestigious annual music festival opening on May 12th with Smetana's *My Country* (*Má vlast*).
* **Theatres:** National Theatre, Estates Theatre, Black Light Theatres, and traditional puppet theatres.
* **Cinemas and Clubs:** Historic cinemas (Lucerna, Bio Oko) and music venues (Roxy, MeetFactory, Reduta Jazz Club).

### City Public Transport
* One of the most efficient, punctual, and affordable transit networks in Europe.
* **Prague Metro:**
  * 3 lines: **Line A (Green)**, **Line B (Yellow)**, **Line C (Red)**.
  * Intersection stations: Můstek, Muzeum, Florenc.
  * New automated **Line D (Blue)** currently under construction.
* **Trams:** Extensive surface network; **Tram 22** is famous for its scenic route past Prague Castle.
* **Other modes:** City buses, trolleybuses, Petřín funicular, and river ferries.

### My Favourite Places in Prague
* **Vyšehrad:** Ancient fortress on a cliff above the Vltava; quiet parks, Romanesque rotunda, and the **Slavín Cemetery** (resting place of Karel Čapek, Antonín Dvořák, Bedřich Smetana).
* **Letná Park (*Letenské sady*):** Great views over the bridges of Prague; famous for the working **Metronome** (site of the former Stalin monument) and skatepark.
* **Riegrovy Sady:** Popular hilltop park in Vinohrady, famous for sunset views of Prague Castle.
* **Riverside (*Náplavka*):** Popular pedestrian promenade along the Vltava with cafes, boat bars, and weekend farmers' markets.

### Practical Tips for Visitors
* Visit Charles Bridge and Prague Castle early in the morning to avoid large tourist crowds.
* Try coffee and traditional desserts in a historic café (Café Louvre, Kavárna Slavia, Café Imperial).
* Explore the maze of passages (*pasáže*) in the New Town and Old Town.
